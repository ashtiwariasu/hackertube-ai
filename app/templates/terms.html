Verdicts, reviews and ratings

Submitting a video

Anyone signed in can submit a public YouTube video that isn't listed here, with a review of it. It must be at least 3 minutes long and allowed to play on other sites, and it can't be age-restricted or live. It gets its own page and shows under New finds, but stays out of rankings, topics and search engines until at least 4 of its first 5 viewers agree with a review of it. Tell us if you made the video or are connected to its creator or were paid to promote it: your review is labelled "Connected to the creator" and never earns. We read the video's details from YouTube and refresh them at least every 30 days, and we can remove any submission.
