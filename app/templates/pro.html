How personalisation works

When you connect YouTube we read your liked videos and subscriptions once, with read-only access. We keep only what the feed needs: which catalogue videos you've liked, which catalogue channels you follow, and a numeric taste profile. We don't keep your access token or your raw YouTube data, and you can delete everything from your account page or revoke access in your Google account settings.

YouTube no longer lets apps read watch history directly, so for history you upload your Google Takeout file. We keep only the matches against the catalogue.
