How to take the screenshot

  1. Open youtube.com/feed/history, or your Liked videos, or Subscriptions.
  2. Take a screenshot. A long one is fine. On a Mac: Shift, Command and 4. On Windows: Windows key, Shift and S. On a phone, screenshot the YouTube app.
  3. Drop it above. Add up to four for a longer history.

What happens to the image

A vision model reads the video titles and channel names, then the image is discarded. We keep the titles we read and which ones matched the catalogue, nothing else. Privacy policy.
