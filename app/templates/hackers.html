Why it can't be gamed

  • Real viewers decide. A review counts only when people who watched the video here agree with it. They aren't partners and they're never paid.
  • Five different people. Each review's {{ panel }} judges must be on {{ panel }} different networks and browsers. One viewer counts on at most {{ judge_per_author }} of your reviews, and people who came through your link can't rate yours.
  • No flooding, by hand or with AI. Paid spots are limited every week. Copied, templated or generic reviews don't earn, and an automatic check holds back spam.
  • One person, one account. One PayPal account per partner. Every payment, and every Diamond pot, is checked by a person before it's sent.

We never pay for watching, liking, sharing or subscribing. Paid reviews carry a "Paid partner" label.
