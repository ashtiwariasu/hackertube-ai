Without an account

Likes and Watch later are stored only in your browser. We keep standard server logs (IP address, pages requested) for security and debugging. Videos play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed.

With Google sign-in

We receive your Google account ID, email, name and profile picture, and store them with your likes and Watch later list so they sync across devices.

YouTube data

If you connect YouTube we request read-only access (youtube.readonly) and read your liked videos and subscriptions once per connect. We store only: the IDs of liked videos that are in our catalogue, the IDs of subscribed channels that are in our catalogue, counts, and a numeric taste vector computed from titles. We do not store your access token, refresh token, raw liked-video list, subscription list, or titles. We do not share YouTube data with anyone or use it for advertising. HackerTube AI uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete this data at any time from your account page, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

HackerTube AI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Connected sources

On the Sources page you can give us a public username or link: GitHub, Hacker News, Bluesky, Mastodon, Stack Overflow, a personal site, or Discord servers through Discord's sign-in. We read public data from that service once, over its public API, when you flip the switch. We keep the username, a one-line summary (such as "120 starred repos"), and a numeric taste vector computed from the text. We do not keep the posts, comments, stars or page text themselves. If you turn on "Find my public profiles from my email", we look up your sign-in email on Gravatar and GitHub's public-email search and show you what we found before using it. Turn any switch off to delete that source's data.

Text from your AI assistant

On the "Ask your AI" page you can paste what ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini says about your interests. It is sent once to Anthropic's Claude API, which turns it into a short list of topics, tools, creators and preferences and is instructed to drop health, financial, relationship, religious, political and location details. We store that short list, not the text you pasted. For people who are not signed in, the list stays in the browser until sign-in. Turn the "Ask your AI" switch off on your Sources page to delete it.

Screenshots

If you upload a screenshot of your YouTube pages, it is resized, stripped of metadata and sent once to Anthropic's Claude API, which reads the video titles and channel names. We do not store the image. Anthropic processes it under its API data policy. We keep the titles that were read and the catalogue videos they matched; for signed-in users they are saved to the account, and for everyone else they stay in the browser until you sign in. Turn the Screenshots switch off on your Sources page to delete them.

Watch history uploads

When you upload a Takeout file we parse it in memory and keep only the catalogue videos it contains, the total count, and a taste vector. The file itself is not stored.

Watching on this site

When you play a video here, the player tells us how far you got. We use that to resume where you left off, to count a video as watched once you've seen most of it, and to steer your picks away from videos you abandon or mark "Not for me". For signed-in users this is stored with the account; for everyone else it stays in the browser. It is never shared.

Verdicts, reviews and ratings

To check that a verdict follows a real watch, the player sends the server a heartbeat while a video plays, and the server signs a small record of how much it saw played. When you give a verdict, write a review or rate one we store your answer, the review, the time, and two salted one-way codes: one for your browser and one for your network (the /24 of your IP address), used only to tell independent readers apart and to stop one person posing as many. We never store your IP address with them. Reviews are public under a name you choose or a random handle; we never show your Google name or email. Review text is checked once by Anthropic's Claude for abuse before it is shown. If you join the Partner Program we keep your application and our decision, the paid spots you claim and when, and your monthly statements and payments. Partners can give us a PayPal email for payments, one per partner; we never ask for bank details on the site. Before paying a partner we check for fake accounts by comparing the browser and network codes of their reviews and ratings and when the rating accounts were made. When you follow a partner's link we set a cookie (ht_ref) holding their handle and the time, for 30 days, so that you can't rate that partner's reviews. Delete your verdicts, reviews and ratings from your account page at any time. If you submit a video, we store its ID, who submitted it and when, and the title, channel, length, view count and publish date we read from YouTube, refreshed at least every 30 days. When you connect YouTube we also note your own channel's ID, if you have one, so your reviews of your own videos never earn.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and PostHog to count visits and see which parts of the site get used (pages, chips, pastes, player progress, purchases, page speed and script errors). PostHog also records how the page is used (clicks, scrolls, cursor movement) so we can fix what confuses people; anything you type or paste, and the results we show you from it, are masked in those recordings. Signed-in users are identified by an internal account id, never by email. Both services set their own cookies or local storage; block them with a content blocker if you prefer.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of payment and your email; we never see your card details.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in Google Cloud (United States). To delete your account entirely, email us from your sign-in address.
